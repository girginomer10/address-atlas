Copy, before and after

Applies to whichever direction wins. Rule: say what a thing is or does, once, in as few words as possible. No slogans, no eyebrow labels, no subtitle under every title.

Sidebar card, beforePrivate by design · Encrypted on this Mac · Credentials and portfolio data stay encrypted before storage or sync.After(removed; covered once in Settings → Privacy)
Portfolio header, beforePortfolio overview / Portfolio / A unified view of wallets, exchange balances, custom tokens, staking, and rewards.AfterTotal value
Scan panel, beforeRefresh portfolio · Scan every saved wallet and exchange · Scan all sources · Encrypted storage ✓ Private sync ✓ Direct connections ✓AfterScan
Add wallet, beforeAdd a wallet · Paste a public address—never a seed phrase or private key · Watch-only access: Address Atlas reads public blockchain data. It cannot sign transactions or move funds.AfterField placeholder: 0x…, bc1…, or any supported address. One line under it: Public addresses only.
Warning box, beforePartial scan warnings · 1 issue · A price source was temporarily unavailable for one token.AfterNEW on Base has no price, so it’s left out of the total.
Welcome, beforeEverything you track. Nothing to hand over.AfterAdd a wallet address or an exchange to see what you hold.
Saved count, beforeSaved wallets · 1 encrypted on this MacAfterWallets
