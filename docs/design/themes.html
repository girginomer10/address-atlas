Address Atlas: round two, in colour

Six colourful, crypto-native directions. Same Portfolio screen and data as round one. Colour does a job in each: it identifies a chain or asset, shows weight, or marks where you are. No purple-to-blue gradients, no glass, no neon glow.

A

Chain colours

Every asset and network wears its own colour: BTC orange, ETH indigo, SOL purple, USDC blue. The interface stays white and quiet so the portfolio itself is the colourful part. Familiar to anyone who uses a modern wallet.
